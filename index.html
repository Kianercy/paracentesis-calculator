Enter mean arterial pressure after volume resuscitation. Formula: (2×Diastolic + Systolic) ÷ 3
3 Disease Activity: Alcohol
Select current alcohol use status. Sustained abstinence dramatically reduces recurrence risk.
4 Disease Activity: Viral Hepatitis
HCV: Check RNA status. HBV: Check DNA level. SVR/suppression reduces risk 40-60%.
